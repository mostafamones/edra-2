# Edra mobile — Android support

Cross-platform direction confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-19.

## Outcome

Deliver Android through the shared React Native instructor companion, with the functionality achieved in the iPhone companion by the time of the port. Use platform-specific modules where Android needs native device integration.

## Scope

The iPhone companion defines the functional scope. React Native owns screens on both platforms and shares workflows across them. Android follows its platform conventions. On iOS, native components preserve the existing appearance and interactions, including system Liquid Glass and the existing behavior on earlier supported OS versions.

Before implementation, identify the iPhone functionality available at that point and use it to produce a concrete parity plan. Platform-specific interactions follow Android conventions while preserving the same product behavior.

## Completion

Staff can carry out the corresponding iPhone companion workflows on Android. The parity plan identifies the functionality being ported and verifies its coverage.

## Delivery tracking

The current Linear target is December 31, 2026.
