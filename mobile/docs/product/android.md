# Edra mobile — Android support

Direction confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-10.

## Outcome

Deliver a native Kotlin Android companion with the functionality achieved in the iPhone companion by the time of the port.

## Scope

The iPhone companion defines the functional scope. Android brings those workflows to its native platform, using Edra’s existing academic rules and services.

Before implementation, identify the iPhone functionality available at that point and use it to produce a concrete parity plan. Platform-specific interactions follow Android conventions while preserving the same product behavior.

## Completion

Staff can carry out the corresponding iPhone companion workflows on Android. The parity plan identifies the functionality being ported and verifies its coverage.

## Delivery tracking

The current Linear target is December 31, 2026.
