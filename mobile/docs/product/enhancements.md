# Edra mobile — instructor companion enhancements

Milestone direction approved by the owner, 2026-10-10. Scope and workflows remain provisional.

## Purpose

Expand Edra’s instructor companion into a place where academy staff can organize teaching, prepare learning, support students and coordinate their work from the phone.

## Users and responsibilities

Owners, Head Admins, Admins and Instructors use the companion according to their academy responsibilities and permissions. It supports both teaching and day-to-day academic administration.

Students and parents benefit through the learning material, academic records, feedback and communication staff manage in Edra.

## Milestone outcome

By the end of this milestone, staff will be able to follow the academy’s activity, manage everyday academic work and act on student needs from the iPhone.

The companion will bring together content units, sessions, assessments and student information, with notes, notifications and focused team discussions supporting follow-up.

## Pillars

1. **A connected academic workspace.** Staff can move from the academy overview to the teaching activity, learning material or student work that needs their attention.
2. **Learning organized through content units.** Preparing and releasing learning on the phone follows the same content-unit structure students use across Edra.
3. **Student support informed by their record.** Participation, performance and academic notes give staff the context to support each student.
4. **Team concerns lead to shared outcomes.** Focused discussions connect a concern, the people involved and its resolution, with the conversation available for later reference.

## Included scope

### 1. Academy overview and teaching organization

- An overview of academy activity and work needing attention.
- A teaching calendar and access to groups, schedules and sessions.
- Managing groups and schedules, and reviewing past sessions and attendance.
- Continuing the session and attendance workflows introduced in the mobile MVP.

### 2. Content units and assessments

- Preparing and editing content units with explanation videos, assignments, quizzes and answer material.
- Releasing content units to their intended students and archiving them.
- Creating and managing assignments, quizzes and exams, including standalone assignments.
- Reviewing submitted work, marking assessments and announcing results.
- Reviewing and correcting official results under the assessment rules.

### 3. Student information and learning access

- Searching the academy’s students and opening their profiles.
- Reviewing student participation, group placement, attendance, replacement attendance and performance.
- Maintaining student details and placement within the staff member’s permissions.
- Reviewing learning access and managing staff grants, revocations and extensions.
- Managing coupons used to provide learning access.
- Viewing student and academy reports and managing parent report links.

### 4. Academic notes

- Reading notes associated with the relevant student or academic activity.
- Creating and editing draft notes, choosing their intended audience and sharing them.
- Correcting shared notes under the same rules used throughout Edra.

### 5. Staff notifications

- An in-app inbox for academy notifications, with read status and links to the relevant work.
- Push notifications that let staff open the relevant academy record or conversation.
- Notification preferences for the academies a staff member belongs to.

### 6. Concern threads and team follow-up

- Owners and Admins opening a thread about a specific academy concern and choosing its participants.
- Conversations with one instructor, several team members or the whole team.
- Settling a concern through an explicit confirmation by an Owner or Admin.
- Participants retaining access to the discussion and recorded outcome after settlement.
- A management view of ongoing and settled concerns for academy leadership.

## Completion criteria

This milestone is complete when these experiences work together:

1. Staff use the academy overview to find upcoming teaching and pending academic work, then open and manage the relevant activity.
2. Staff prepare and release a content unit from the phone, then review its students’ work and announce assessment results.
3. Staff find a student, review their participation and performance, and manage the relevant placement, access, notes or parent report.
4. Staff receive an academy notification and open the associated work from the app.
5. An Owner or Admin raises a concern with the relevant team members, they discuss it, and its settlement remains available as shared history.

## Scope boundaries

This milestone extends the iOS staff companion. Android support and the mobile student portal remain separate milestones.

Academy creation, staff invitation and role administration, academic-year setup and closure, and bulk student import remain web workflows in this proposed milestone.

Concern threads cover academy team follow-up. General social messaging, student or parent chat, and task-management features are outside this milestone.

Approval of this direction does not settle the detailed workflows or authorize implementation. Those decisions will be made with the additional product and application context available at that time.
