# Edra mobile MVP — iOS instructor companion

Approved by the owner, 2026-10-10.

## Purpose

Edra’s instructor companion brings classroom work to the iPhone. Staff can run sessions, record attendance and mark physical work while moving around the academy, with that work becoming part of the same academic record students and parents follow.

## Users and responsibilities

The companion serves academy staff carrying out teaching and academic work. Owners, Head Admins, Admins and Instructors use the workflows permitted by their academy role.

Students and parents follow the resulting attendance and announced results through Edra’s student experience and parent reports.

## Milestone outcome

By the end of this milestone, staff will be able to start a teaching session, take and finalize attendance, and record and announce assignment marks from their iPhone.

The app will connect session work and marking through clear navigation, supporting staff who work across more than one academy.

## Pillars

1. **Classroom work from the phone.** Staff can start a session and take attendance where teaching happens, and use the camera to identify students when handling physical work.
2. **One academic record across Edra.** Attendance and marks entered on the phone belong to the same student records used by the web application, student learning and parent reports.
3. **Official academic actions carry the same meaning everywhere.** Finalized attendance drives learning access, reports and parent communication. Announced results become the student’s official marks and contribute to performance.

## Included scope

### 1. Staff access and navigation

- Staff sign-in and academy selection.
- Moving between sessions, attendance and assignment marking within the selected academy.
- Access according to the staff member’s academy role.

### 2. Live teaching and attendance

- Viewing available schedules and starting a session.
- Opening live sessions and their student lists.
- Recording and reviewing attendance through manual selection or student-code scanning.
- Handling unexpected and replacement attendance under the academy’s attendance rules.
- Ending sessions and finalizing attendance as separate actions.

### 3. Physical assignment marking

- Finding assignments that still need marking, including those whose due date has passed.
- Selecting a student manually or identifying them by scanning their code.
- Recording and adjusting marks for assignment parts, and reviewing the student’s recorded work.
- Saving marks for review and announcing complete results for individual students or a selected group.
- Marking assignments within content units and standalone assignments.

Content units remain the core of online learning. Mobile marking contributes to their assessments and to independently used assignments; it does not determine when learning content becomes available to students.

## Completion criteria

The mobile MVP is complete when these experiences work together:

1. A staff member signs in, chooses an academy and moves between its live teaching and marking workflows.
2. Staff select a schedule, start a session, record attendance manually or by scanning, and finalize its attendance from the phone.
3. Staff select an assignment, identify students and record their part marks while working through physical assignments.
4. Staff review and announce results from the phone. Those results appear in the student’s performance and parent reports alongside work recorded through the web application.

## Scope boundaries

This milestone delivers the native iPhone instructor companion. Assignment creation and content-unit preparation and release remain web workflows in this milestone.

Broader staff tools, including academy overviews, student directories, notes, notifications and concern threads, belong to the instructor companion enhancements milestone. Native Android support and the mobile student portal have their own milestones.

## Delivery tracking

Linear tracks the milestone’s work and targets. Academic domain documents define attendance and assessment rules; mobile engineering and integration references define their native implementation.
