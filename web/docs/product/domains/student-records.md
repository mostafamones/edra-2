# Edra — student records and annual participation

Approved by the owner, 2026-10-10. Detailed enrollment, matching and access rules remain for their separate reviews.

## Student identity and academic history

An academy student profile represents one student's relationship with that academy across academic years. Each year in which the student participates has an annual student record under that profile.

The annual record contains the student's academy-entered name and contacts, level and optional track, optional primary group, participation type and archive status. Attendance, assessed work, results, notes and reports refer to the relevant annual participation.

A student may have records in several academies. Each academy maintains its own information and history. Within an academy, a student profile has at most one annual record for a given year.

## Creating and maintaining records

Staff can create student records before students have online accounts. Enrollment and imports also produce academy records through their reviewed flows.

Creating a genuinely new student establishes their academy profile and annual participation. A returning student keeps their existing profile and receives participation for the new year. Earlier attendance, work and results remain associated with their original year.

Public booking also permits signed-out returning registration without account connection. A unique historical student-phone match reuses the academy profile for the new annual record; ambiguous profiles or an existing participation under another phone require staff resolution. This record-creation match never proves account ownership or changes an existing account connection. Public registration uses its configured current-year fields and preserves prior records.

Changes to a student's current details do not replace historical session rosters or official academic records.

## Contacts

Staff records require the student's name, a valid Egyptian student mobile number and a valid Egyptian parent or guardian mobile number. Entering a contact does not verify ownership of that phone.

A student phone may appear only once within an academy and academic year, including archived records. Shared names and parent phones are permitted; they do not by themselves identify the same student.

Academy-entered information remains preserved when an online account is connected. Account information does not silently overwrite the academy's values.

The academy record name is the primary student display name throughout that academy. For a genuinely new online student, onboarding initializes it from the optional account display name, falling back to the full account name. Staff may then edit it independently. Account-name changes do not overwrite academy records. See [personal account settings](account-settings.md) for the agreed name precedence and linked/suspended table indicator; student profile pictures do not appear in staff student-record views.

## Placement

Every annual student record has a level and may have a track. Together these are called the student's program.

A primary group is optional. An unassigned student still has an academy record and can participate in the permitted academic workflows. A selected group must belong to the same academy and year and be compatible with the student's level and track.

Removing a track follows the approved academic-structure rules: affected students retain their level and lose that track placement. Their groups become level-wide while historical references remain intact.

Changing a student's level, track or primary group is a current-placement action. Past participation remains represented by the session and academic records that captured it.

## Participation type

The annual record identifies the student's normal participation as in-person or online-only.

In-person students may also use online learning. An online-only student may attend an eligible in-person session without changing their normal participation type.

Participation type, online account connection and learning access are separate. Having an account does not turn an in-person student into an online-only student or automatically grant access to all material.

## Online accounts

New students gain online academy access through its student invite and onboarding flow. Signed-in returning students may also use the public booking link to continue through returning onboarding and gain academy access without another invite. Signed-in students with eligible current-year records may use that booking link for verified existing-record connection too. These flows preserve the existing academy identity and connect the account through onboarding without staff manually attaching it.

Signing in with a matching phone alone does not grant academy access. Staff student management does not connect accounts. Students without connected accounts can still have attendance, marks, notes and parent reports.

Student codes identify records for academy workflows such as attendance and marking. They are not sign-in credentials.

## Archival and restoration

Archiving removes a student record from ordinary active student lists while retaining its contacts, placement and academic history. Authorized staff can find archived records and restore them within the applicable academic-year rules.

An archived record's phone remains reserved within that academy and year. Creating another record with that phone is not a substitute for reviewing or restoring the existing record.

Archiving annual participation also adds online-access suspension caused by archival. Restoring that participation removes only the archival suspension. An independent staff suspension, whether applied before or during archival, remains until explicitly removed by staff. Neither action disconnects the account or removes academic history.

## Staff permissions

Owners, Head Admins, Admins and Instructors can read academy student records and contacts, create validated records with initial placement, and edit names and contact details.

Owners, Head Admins and Admins can change existing placement and participation type, archive and restore records, import students and make permitted bulk group changes. Instructors do not perform these administrative changes.

All roles use the same contact validation. There is no staff-role bypass for missing or invalid required phones.

## Related workflows

Enrollment, account matching, import review, student codes and learning access have their own detailed rules. Student maintenance does not merge records, create authentication accounts or decide a student's content access through contact edits alone.
