# Edra — report metrics and history

Pre-approved working draft, 2026-10-11. The owner will re-review it with the master thread before implementation planning. These proposed formulas and closed-year link policies remain decisions to settle, not final implementation requirements.

## Attendance

I recommend calculating mandatory attendance as:

**(Present + Late) ÷ (Present + Late + Absent)**

Use finalized records only. Show Excused and Unknown separately, excluding both from the denominator. If there are no qualifying records, show no rate rather than zero percent. Optional-session participation is separate.

Confirmed session-archival rule, 2026-10-13: archived sessions are excluded from attendance totals, rates and absence counts. Restoration makes their existing finalized attendance count again. This exclusion is approved independently of the draft formula and historical-report recommendations in this document.

Replacement attendance shows recovery of missed material without removing the original absence or changing the original attendance rate.

## Assessment performance

Show each announced result with its score, maximum and percentage. Group summaries by assignments, quizzes and exams rather than combining everything into one unexplained average.

Missing or unmarked work does not count as zero. A recorded zero does. Exact aggregate weighting and treatment of incomplete or invalidated attempts remain decisions for the assessment/reporting rules.

Graphs should use the same approved calculations as the displayed summaries. Their exact measures and time grouping need confirmation before implementation.

## Historical reports

I recommend keeping closed-year reports readable through their own revocable links. New-year reports use separate links; closing a year does not redirect an old link to a new annual record.

Academic history remains preserved even when a parent link is revoked. Student archival and parent-link availability need an explicit policy.
