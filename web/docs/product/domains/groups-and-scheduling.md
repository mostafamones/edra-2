# Edra — groups and scheduling

Approved by the owner, 2026-10-11. Detailed follow-ups remain in the separate planning review notes.

## Groups and placement

A group organizes students who normally attend together within one academy and academic year. It belongs to a level and may target a particular track within that level.

A student has at most one current primary group and may remain unassigned. A level-wide group accepts students from that level, with or without a track. A track-specific group accepts students from the same level and track.

Owners, Head Admins and Admins create and manage groups and change existing student placement. Instructors can view groups and their students, and set initial placement when creating a student record under their approved permissions.

Moving a student changes their current primary group. Earlier session rosters and attendance retain the group information recorded at the time. The student may attend permitted replacement or shared sessions outside their primary group.

## Group capacity

Groups may have a configured capacity. Capacity counts non-archived current-year students whose primary group is that group. A group without a capacity has no enrollment limit.

The public booking form enforces available places. Owner, Head Admin and Admin may explicitly place a student beyond capacity through staff management. Lowering capacity does not remove students already placed in the group.

Capacity is an enrollment constraint, not a cap on attendance at optional or replacement sessions. It does not reserve places for students who have not completed registration.

## Group changes and archival

Group names, notes and capacity may be maintained without rewriting academic history. A used group's level or track is not changed through an ordinary group edit; changing placement requires an explicit reviewed operation.

Before archiving a group, staff resolve the student placements and schedule references that still depend on it. Archival stops new use and retains history. Restoration is available in the active year when the group remains compatible with current offerings.

The approved track-removal flow is a specific exception: affected groups become level-wide and retain their identity and students. It does not combine separate groups into one.

## Schedules

A schedule describes when teaching is planned, where it takes place and which students it targets. A session is the actual meeting started from that plan.

A schedule can target one group, an entire level, or a level and track. A level audience includes all its tracks. Individual student selections are outside the MVP schedule audience model.

## Recurring and one-off schedules

A recurring schedule has weekly meeting slots that repeat within its academic year. Each slot specifies a weekday, start time and end time. The schedule may also specify a location.

A one-off schedule contains one or more explicitly dated meetings. These meetings do not repeat weekly. Each has its own date, start time and end time.

Recurring or one-off timing is separate from whether attendance is mandatory or optional. A one-off meeting can be mandatory, and a recurring meeting can be optional.

## Mandatory and optional attendance

For mandatory meetings, the roster identifies students expected to attend. Finalized attendance applies the academy's attendance and communication rules.

For optional meetings, the roster identifies eligible students. Students can attend without a separate session booking; non-attendance does not produce a negative absence consequence or parent absence message.

The session rules define exactly which students enter the roster, including participation type and archive status.

## Staff permissions

Owners, Head Admins and Admins create, edit and archive recurring and one-off schedules.

Instructors may create one-off schedules and edit any one-off schedule in the academy. They may archive only one-off schedules they created. They do not manage recurring schedules.

Editing a schedule affects future teaching plans. Existing session rosters and official attendance remain intact; changes to an actual session follow the session-change rules.

## Track removal

Recurring schedules targeting a removed track become level-wide. One-off schedules preserve their original track targeting through its historical name. Earlier session snapshots are unchanged.

How a future meeting derives its roster from a historical track target is defined in the session rules before implementation.
