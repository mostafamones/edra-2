# Edra — accounts, academy membership and staff

Approved by the owner, 2026-10-10.

## Accounts and academy membership

An Edra account belongs to a person and can be used across academies. The person’s relationship to each academy determines what they can do there.

A staff membership gives an account access to one academy under a defined role. A person may hold different staff roles in different academies. Choosing an academy opens that academy’s workspace and uses the permissions of that membership.

A student’s academy record holds their academic relationship and history with that academy. It can exist before the student has an online account. New students gain online access through the academy’s student invite and onboarding flow. Signed-in returning students may use the public booking link to continue through returning onboarding and receive academy access without another invite. A matching phone number alone does not grant academy access. Staff student management does not link accounts.

## Academy ownership

Each academy has one Owner. The account that creates the academy becomes its Owner. An account may own one academy in the MVP and may also belong to other academies as staff.

Ownership transfer is outside the MVP. Normal staff-management actions cannot remove, suspend or demote the Owner.

## Staff roles

- **Owner:** leads the academy and holds its highest authority over staff, operations and academic direction.
- **Head Admin:** acts with the Owner’s authority in academy operations, except for actions reserved to the Owner.
- **Admin:** manages day-to-day administration and academic operations within the role’s permissions.
- **Instructor:** contributes to teaching and student support, prepares learning material and assessments, and follows participation and progress. The permission matrix defines the actions available within those responsibilities.

These are fixed academy roles. Custom roles and individual permission overrides are outside the MVP.

## Owner-reserved actions

Only the Owner may delete or close the academy, appoint or remove a Head Admin, change a Head Admin’s role, or suspend and restore a Head Admin’s access.

Head Admins may manage Admins and Instructors. Admins may invite and manage Instructors. Instructors do not manage staff memberships.

An academy may have more than one Head Admin. Head Admins cannot change each other’s authority or their own role through staff-management actions.

Domain permissions distinguish ordinary academy settings from any additional Owner-reserved settings. Being the Owner does not permit bypassing academic history or access rules.

## Staff invitations

An authorized staff member invites a person using their phone number and the intended role. The recipient signs in with an account whose verified phone matches the invitation and accepts it to join the academy.

An invitation is valid for seven days and may be revoked before acceptance. An academy has at most one pending invitation for a given phone number. Accepting an invitation does not change the role of someone who is already a member.

Owners and Head Admins can review and manage invitations for the staff roles they may appoint. Admins can review the academy’s invitations, but may copy or revoke only Instructor invitations they created.

An invitation cannot be accepted if its creator no longer has permission to appoint that role.

## Changes to staff access

Role changes determine the member’s permissions within that academy. Suspension temporarily blocks staff access while retaining the membership for restoration. Removal ends the staff membership; returning requires a new invitation.

Changes to staff access preserve the academy’s academic records and the attribution of earlier work. Removing a membership does not delete the person’s Edra account or affect their memberships in other academies.

Staff management does not permit self-removal, self-suspension or changing one’s own role. Any future leave-academy flow is a separate product decision.

## Related domain rules

The permission matrix defines academic actions such as content release, marking, attendance finalization and access management. This document defines membership and staff administration; it does not assign all academic permissions merely from a role’s description.
