# Edra — student enrollment and account connection

Approved by the owner, 2026-10-10. Detailed account-change, intake and access workflows remain for their separate reviews.

## Joining an academy

A new student gains online academy access through its student invite link. The link opens that academy’s student onboarding flow, where the student signs in or creates their Edra account and completes any required student setup. A signed-in returning student may also enter through the academy's public booking link without requiring another invite.

The flow connects an existing academy record or enrolls a genuinely new student. It preserves the student’s academic identity and earlier work.

Signing in outside the invite flow does not join an academy merely because a phone matches its records. Once connected, a student can return through their normal academy navigation without using the invite each time.

## Student invites

The academy has a shareable student invite link for its active enrollment. Owners, Head Admins and Admins manage enrollment availability and replace or revoke the link. Instructors may share the active link.

Replacing or revoking a link prevents new entry through that link. It does not disconnect students who have already joined.

The link identifies the academy. Access to an individual student record still depends on the account’s verified identity and the enrollment rules.

## Existing students

During onboarding, the system compares the account’s verified student phone with student phones on eligible records in the academy’s active year.

When there is one matching, non-archived record and no conflicting account connection, the account connects automatically to that student’s academy profile. The existing annual participation, placement, attendance, work, marks and parent report remain intact.

The student does not complete a second enrollment form for a record already present. Connecting an account does not replace staff-entered contacts or change the student’s level, track, group or participation type.

A parent phone alone does not choose a student record when the same parent contact is shared by siblings.

## New students

When no existing record matches, the invite flow offers enrollment if the academy is accepting new students.

The student supplies the required name and contacts and chooses from the academy’s available levels, tracks, participation options and groups. Group placement may remain unassigned where the enrollment configuration permits it.

The student reviews the information before submitting. A valid submission creates their academy profile and annual participation and connects their account within the onboarding flow. Ordinary valid enrollment does not require a separate staff approval step.

If the academy is not accepting new enrollment, the flow explains how to contact staff. It does not create a record merely because the student opened an invite.

## Returning students

Returning students previously participated in this academy and are joining its new academic year without a current-year record.

For a signed-in returning student, the public booking link serves as the academy invite. It skips the public form and opens the same online academy onboarding used by invited students.

The student chooses their current academy program—level and track where applicable—and group. The school level selected during account setup does not replace this academy program choice.

Completing onboarding creates the new annual record under their existing academy profile, connects their account and grants online academy access without a separately sent invite. Their in-person or online-only participation type and earlier history are preserved.

Previous-year placement and learning access do not carry over automatically. A student who already has a current-year record follows the existing-student flow rather than returning enrollment.

## Duplicates and connection conflicts

One account may connect to one student profile within an academy, and one student profile may connect to one account. The account may connect to records in other academies independently.

Existing phone uniqueness includes archived annual records. An archived or already-connected match directs the student to an appropriate resolution path; it is not treated as permission to create a duplicate.

The flow does not merge profiles or replace another account’s connection automatically. Staff may review academy information, correct permitted contact details or restore a record. The student then completes the connection through onboarding; staff do not manually attach an account.

Shared parent phones and similar names do not alone block enrollment or cause records to be merged.

## After connection

The student can navigate the academy and see the learning, performance and other information their annual participation and access permit.

Connection is separate from entitlement to learning material. Attendance, coupons, staff grants, submissions and announced results continue to control their respective learning access.

Student codes remain academy identifiers for workflows such as attendance and marking. They do not authenticate an account or replace the invite flow.

## Related rules

Account verification and recovery, enrollment capacity, contact changes, access suspension and disputed account connections have their own detailed rules. This flow preserves the approved record model and academy boundaries.
