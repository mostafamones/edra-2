# Edra — public booking form

Approved by the owner, 2026-10-11; booking settings, guest returning registration, booking-link account connection and manager-controlled year start confirmed 2026-10-25. This document covers public booking and its student entry behavior.

## Purpose

The academy shares an open form that anyone can use to register for its current academic year. An Edra account is not required. When someone is signed in, Edra uses their existing information and academy relationship to provide the appropriate registration experience.

## Form settings

Owners, Head Admins and Admins configure the form and open or close it. Instructors may view and share the active form.

Every current-year level, track and group is available through the form by default; staff exclude the ones the form should not offer and set any participation choices. The form shows students only the choices that are not excluded, including offerings added after the settings were saved.

The settings record excluded current-year levels, tracks and groups and specify whether registration without a group is allowed. Staff either fix participation to one type or let students choose between in-person and online-only. A fixed type is applied without asking the student to choose it. A new year's booking starts closed; staff configure valid choices before opening it. A level with active tracks still requires a compatible track that is not excluded.

## Information collected

The form collects:

- Student name.
- Student contact number.
- Parent or guardian contact number.
- Level.
- Track and group, where those choices are enabled.
- Participation type, where staff expose that choice.

The form uses the app’s normal validation for required information, phone numbers and compatible level, track and group choices.

## Submitting the form

A successful submission adds the student to the academy’s current-year student records. Staff do not need to approve every submission.

If the student contact number already exists in that academy and academic year, the form reports that it is already registered and does not create another record. This includes numbers on archived records.

Students may share a parent contact number or have the same name. Those details alone do not prevent registration.

Returning students may also register while signed out, without creating or linking an Edra account. When the student phone identifies exactly one historical academy profile and that profile has no current-year record, the new annual record reuses that profile. Public form fields and booking settings govern the new annual record; earlier records and any existing account connection remain unchanged. Ambiguous historical profiles or a profile already participating under another phone require staff resolution rather than creating or merging identities. Public responses do not reveal historical record details. Entering a phone for registration never establishes account ownership.

## Signed-in students

For a signed-in student joining this academy for the first time, the form prefills their known account details. The student supplies the remaining information and academy choices enabled by the form settings. Submitting creates their academy record; online academy access still requires the student invite flow.

For a signed-in student with an eligible current-year record, the booking link also serves as an academy invite. Edra uses the same verified identity matching and confirmation as student-invite onboarding to connect their account to that record without a separately sent invite. It does not create another annual record, ask for placement again, or change staff-entered details, placement or participation type. An already-connected student can open the academy. Archived records and conflicting account connections retain the normal staff-resolution rules.

Returning students are students from an earlier academic year who are joining the academy for the new year and do not yet have a current-year record.

When a returning student is signed in, the public booking link serves as their academy invite. Edra skips the public form and opens the same online academy onboarding used by invited students. Their school level stays fixed to the level on their account; they choose an academy track within that level when tracks are offered and a compatible group, with registration without a group available when booking settings permit it. If the academy does not offer that level, this onboarding cannot create the new-year record. Completing onboarding creates their new-year record under their existing academy profile, connects their account and makes the academy available in their navigation. Connection does not itself grant learning entitlements. Registering before teaching starts does not automatically suspend the student.

This flow preserves the student's in-person or online-only participation type and earlier academic history. Online academy onboarding does not change an in-person student into an online-only student. As confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-19, this academy onboarding does not offer an independent school-level choice. The account level determines the offered track/group choices; staff placement authority and existing records remain unchanged. Access to individual learning material continues to follow the academy's learning-access rules.

New annual participation entered through the booking link follows booking availability and enabled choices independently of the ordinary student invite and its new-enrollment setting. A returning student's preserved participation type and account school level remain authoritative in signed-in onboarding. Existing-current-year connection does not change placement or require those stored choices to remain available for new booking.

## Before teaching starts

The academy's single open year may accept booking and account connection before managers start its student learning. Connected students can see the academy and their registration while learning is not started. Owners, Head Admins and Admins explicitly start the year; calendar dates do not automatically activate learning. Starting the year makes learning eligible under its normal publication, grants, assessment and suspension rules. It does not issue access or clear independent suspension. This applies to all students in the year, including those joining through an ordinary invite. See [academic structure](academic-structure.md) and [learning access](learning-access.md).

## Group availability

Where a group has a configured capacity, the public form offers places only while capacity is available. If the selected group becomes full before submission, the student chooses another available option. Registration without a group is possible when the form settings permit it.

## Staff access

Authorized staff can view submissions and maintain the resulting student records using their existing student-management permissions.

## Closing the form

Closing the form stops new submissions. Students already registered remain in the academy’s records, and earlier submissions remain available to staff.

Closure blocks new annual registration from both the public form and booking-origin returning onboarding. The still-valid booking link remains usable for verified connection to an eligible existing current-year record. Ordinary student-invite availability is independent. Closing booking neither suspends connected students nor starts or stops learning for the year.

## Account onboarding

The student invite and account-onboarding flow is documented separately. Public registration creates a record without granting online academy access to a new student. Signed-in returning and eligible current-year students can connect through the booking link as described above. Account connection and academy visibility do not themselves grant access to individual learning material.
